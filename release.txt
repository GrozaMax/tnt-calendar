⏰ <b>Workout Reminders</b>

You will now receive automatic reminders before your scheduled workouts!
- By default, you'll be reminded 60 minutes before the start time.
- You can customize this time (30, 60, 90, or 120 minutes) right in your ⚙️ <b>Settings</b>.
- Reminders will respect your global notification preferences.
- The <b>Settings</b> button has also been moved to the main menu for easier access!
